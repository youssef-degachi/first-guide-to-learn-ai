# Model landscape: source review

Reviewed: **15 November 2026**. Scope: the introductory ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini map, model-family descriptions, and product-access distinctions in lesson 05 and section 3 of the course guide. This is a documentary review, not a hands-on comparison. Other course sections retain their stated research dates.

| Claim or distinction | Primary source | Evidence type and limits |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI catalog names and intended roles: GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Luna | [API model catalog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models), accessed 15 November | Provider positioning; does not establish a universal ChatGPT picker or independent performance ranking. |
| Chat, Work, Codex, and API access must be distinguished | [Product model guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models), accessed 15 November | Product documentation. Recommendations for Work/Codex should not be presented as Chat availability. |
| Choose a model against the actual task | [Model selection](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/model-selection), accessed 15 November | Provider guidance; the proposed document exercise has not been run as part of this edit. |
| Claude is Anthropic's assistant; Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, and Fable have different intended roles | [Claude product overview](https://claude.com/product/overview) and [model overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview), accessed 15 November | Product documentation and provider recommendations, rather than measured results in this course. |
| Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 have different access arrangements | [Fable/Mythos announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2), published 9 November 2026, accessed 15 November | Mythos verification for qualifying cybersecurity and life-sciences work; do not imply general assistant access. |
| Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon target different workloads | [Carbon release](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/), accessed 15 November | Provider positioning. App, API, and Antigravity availability must be checked separately. |
