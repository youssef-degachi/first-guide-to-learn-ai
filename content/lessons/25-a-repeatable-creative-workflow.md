## The idea

A creative workflow becomes more dependable when its factual core stays fixed. Begin with a verified fact sheet, then create a script, visual brief, and voice passage. Check consistency at every handoff.

### Keep a factual spine

Store the approved date, price, owner, and unknowns in a small source-linked table. Use it as context for every derived format. If a fact changes, identify the artifacts that depend on it.

### Separate creative choices

Tone, composition, pacing, and transitions are editable decisions. They should not quietly change facts or add promises. Give each stage a narrow brief and named invariants.

### Review the assembled lesson

The script may be correct while the caption or voice uses the old date. Inspect the complete artifact as well as its individual components. Save the manual edits so your comparison reflects the whole workflow.

## Try it

**Time/access:** 25–35 minutes; Text.

Give the approved fact sheet from Lab 3. Request a short lesson script for beginners, then request a social caption and a learner worksheet from the same facts.

**Check:** Date, price, owner, and policy status remain consistent across all three. The script should teach one idea rather than cram in a tool list.

**Save/teach:** A fact-to-three-formats demonstration. Record what you edited manually and why.

## Check your work
