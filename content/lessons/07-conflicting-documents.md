## The idea

The supplied launch documents are deliberately inconsistent. One is a proposal, one is an approved decision, and one is an operational note. The useful skill is deciding which claim governs and explaining the others, while leaving missing policy unanswered.

### Work with the source pack

[Open the fictional source pack](downloads/learning-kit/practice-inputs/source-pack.md). Read the authority and date on each document. Ask for a table containing the question, answer, source ID, and unresolved uncertainty.

### The authority rule

D02 explicitly approves a decision and supersedes D01. D03 is newer, but points back to D02. Recency alone is therefore insufficient.

### Missing evidence

Do not infer a refund period from a price or launch date. A plausible business policy is not a policy established by these documents.

## Try it

**Time/access:** 20–30 minutes; Text or file upload.

Supply D01–D03 and the five questions in the source pack. First ask for an answer without an authority rule; then repeat with the instruction to distinguish proposal, approved decision, and operational note.

**Check:** The approved date and price must come from D02. The explanation must account for the older proposal. “Use the newest date in any document” is not an adequate general rule.

**Save/teach:** A decision table showing the conflict and resolution. If both attempts succeed, report that; do not manufacture a dramatic failure.

## Check your work

Check approved facts against D02 and account for the old proposal. Require an explicit unknown for the missing policy.
