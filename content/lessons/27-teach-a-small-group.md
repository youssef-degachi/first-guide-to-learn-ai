## The idea

A learner repeating your words is different from a learner applying the method. Demonstrate once, then let them attempt a comparable task without taking over. Observe where they need help and revise the lesson.

A suggested 60-minute small-group lesson:

| Time | Activity | Your role |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Ask learners to predict an outcome | Reveal their assumptions without correcting everything immediately |
| 5–15 min | Explain one idea with a shared example | Use plain language and a visual |
| 15–25 min | Demonstrate the task and its check | Show the setup, output, and evidence |
| 25–40 min | Learners attempt a comparable task | Observe; avoid taking over the keyboard |
| 40–50 min | Compare results and investigate failures | Separate conceptual confusion from tool/access problems |
| 50–60 min | Teach-back and next exercise | Ask each learner to explain the check and one limitation |

For assessment, award 0–2 each for understanding the task, providing context, producing a usable result, verifying it, and explaining limitations. Keep essential requirements separate from the score. A learner who gets a different output may still demonstrate strong reasoning by finding and correcting its defect.

Use fictional data initially. Introduce learners' real tasks later, after agreeing what information they should share and what actions the tool is permitted to take. This keeps the early lesson focused on the method.

## Try it

**Time/access:** 60–90 minutes preparation plus a 20-minute session; any completed lab.

Choose a topic you have tested. Prepare a short explanation, source note, demonstration, learner exercise, expected answer, and limitation. Teach one learner without taking over their attempt.

**Check:** Can they reproduce the key result and explain why it works? Use an unseen but comparable example to test transfer. Ask what remained confusing and revise the lesson.

**Save/teach:** A lesson pack and an honest report of learner outcomes. This is your readiness check for publishing an educational episode on the topic.

## Check your work
